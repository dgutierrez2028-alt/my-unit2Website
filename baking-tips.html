Baking tips for beginners!

If you are new to baking, here are some tips that I wish I knew when I was first starting to bake! If you want more tips, here is another link, More tips

Tip 1: Chill your dough

Chilling your dough for atleast 30 mintues enhances flavor and prevents spreding when baking.

Tip 2: Dont over mix your dough

Over mixing your dough can cause your cookies to end up being tough, dense, or cakey, when you would want chewy cookies

Tip 3: Dont over bake your cookies

Remove the cookies when the edges are set and the center looks undercooked, the cookies will finish cooking on the hot tray

Tip 4: Try a tester cookie

The way you roll your dough matters. A tall ball of dough will stay tall. You can add one tester cookie in the oven to see how that looks. If it looks good to you then you know what to do. If it looks bad, then you can try to make it more to your liking.

Tip 5: Taste enhancers

Adding sea salt and using more brown sugar than white, make your cookies taste even better
